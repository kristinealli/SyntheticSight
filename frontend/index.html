Scope matters

A strong benchmark result is not universal deepfake detection.

Synthetic Sight was trained on a narrow, still-image task: distinguishing real FFHQ faces from StyleGAN-generated faces. Its performance should not be assumed to generalize to newer generative models, face swaps, video frames, screenshots, heavily compressed images, or other out-of-distribution inputs.
